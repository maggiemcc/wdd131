WDD 131 Site Plan

The Site Name & Purpose

Adventure Powersports

I chose the name "Adventure Powersports" because motorcycles are often associated with going on adventures or traveling. The website is meant to provide people with information on maintenance services provided for vehicles and showcase and sell motorcycles.

Scenarios

  • What vehicle repair services does Adventure Powersports offer?
  • How can I schedule an appointment to get my vehicle repaired?
  • What are some machines they have for sale?

Color Schema

Primary Color: Black (#000) will be used for the header and footer background colors and for text.

Seconday Color: White (#fff) will be the background color for the main section content and used as the font color for the header and footer.

Accent Color: Orange (#D16500) will be used for hover content, some headings, and buttons.

Typography

Heading Font: Londrina Solid

Will be used for the navigation, headings, and titles.

Body Font: Verdana

Will be used for general content such as paragraphs.
